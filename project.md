# 🍽️ FoodLoop — Smart Food Rescue & Community Resource Platform
> **LoopLearn Hackathon 2026 · Problem Statement PS-04**  
> **SDGs**: Zero Hunger (SDG 2), Sustainable Cities (SDG 11), Responsible Consumption (SDG 12), Climate Action (SDG 13)

---

## 📌 Problem Statement Overview
Food waste and food insecurity are two sides of the same tragedy. Commercial food providers (restaurants, cafeterias, bakeries, grocery stores) generate tons of edible surplus daily, which ends up in landfills producing methane gas. Concurrently, community organizations (food banks, shelters, soup kitchens) struggle with limited resources to feed vulnerable populations.

**FoodLoop** bridges this gap through an AI-powered smart matching and redistribution ecosystem that connects surplus food providers with verified community organizations, ensuring food is safely rescued and shared before expiration.

---

## 🚀 Key Platform Capabilities
1. **Surplus Food Donation Hub**: Real-time batch listing with photo analysis, portion tracking, and Good Samaritan legal compliance.
2. **Community Relief Portal**: Radius-based food discovery, 1-click claim requests, and scheduled temperature-controlled transport.
3. **Municipal & Admin Governance**: Citywide zero-waste tracking, organization credential verification, and UN SDG analytics.
4. **AI-Powered Matching & Safety**: Expiry forecasting, route optimization, and RAG-based food safety guidance.
5. **Barakah & Impact Tracking**: Quantifying meals saved, CO2 diverted, and community blessings.
