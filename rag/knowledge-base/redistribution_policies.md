# Food Redistribution Legal Protections and Community Policies

## 1. Legal Protections: The Bill Emerson Good Samaritan Food Donation Act
- **Statutory Protection**: Protects individuals, restaurants, grocery stores, caterers, and corporations from civil and criminal liability when donating food in good faith to non-profit organizations for distribution to needy individuals.
- **Gross Negligence Standard**: Donors and recipient organizations are fully shielded from liability except in cases of gross negligence or intentional misconduct.
- **Past-Date Items Protection**: Food that is past its quality-based manufacturer date ("Best If Used By") remains fully eligible for donation protection provided the item is wholesome and uncompromised.

## 2. Organization Verification Requirements
- All recipient entities on FoodLoop must be verified 501(c)(3) non-profit charities, certified soup kitchens, registered shelters, or authenticated religious/community relief organizations.
- Organizations must maintain clean food transport containers, temperature-controlled transit bags, and sanitary staging environments.
- Organizations must never resell, barter, or trade donated surplus for money or services.

## 3. Donor and Volunteer Code of Conduct
- Maintain transparency regarding ingredients, preparation time, and allergens.
- Promptly report any packaging breaches, off-odors, or temperature excursions.
- Treat all community members with dignity, respect, and confidentiality.
