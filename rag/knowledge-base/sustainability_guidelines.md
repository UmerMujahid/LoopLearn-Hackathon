# FoodLoop Sustainability Framework & UN SDG Alignment

## 1. United Nations Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs)
FoodLoop directly drives progress across 4 key UN Sustainable Development Goals:
- **SDG 2: Zero Hunger (Target 2.1 & 2.2)**: Ensuring universal access to safe, nutritious, and sufficient food all year round by connecting commercial food surplus with shelters and food relief kitchens.
- **SDG 11: Sustainable Cities and Communities (Target 11.6)**: Reducing urban environmental impact and municipal landfill waste burdens by creating hyper-local closed-loop food sharing networks.
- **SDG 12: Responsible Consumption and Production (Target 12.3)**: Halving per capita global food waste at retail and consumer levels and reducing food losses along production and supply chains by 2030.
- **SDG 13: Climate Action (Target 13.2)**: Mitigating greenhouse gas emissions (specifically methane gas generated by decomposing organic landfill waste).

## 2. Standardized Greenhouse Gas (GHG) Conversion Metrics
- **Carbon Offset Factor**: 1 kilogram (kg) of diverted solid food waste prevents approximately **2.5 kg of CO₂ equivalent (CO₂e)** greenhouse gas emissions.
- **Meal Equivalence Standard**: 1 standardized meal portion equals approximately **0.4 kg** of edible food.
- **Methane Potency**: Methane emissions from organic matter rotting in anaerobic landfills are 28 times more potent at trapping atmospheric heat than carbon dioxide over a 100-year timescale.

## 3. Circular Economy Principles
- Keeping resources in active use for as long as possible.
- Extracting maximum nutritional value from harvested produce before composting.
- Regenerating natural systems by replacing landfill dumping with community redistribution and bio-composting.
