# Food Safety and Handling Guidelines for FoodLoop Redistribution

## 1. Temperature Control and Storage Limits
- **Cold Holding**: All perishable prepared foods, dairy, poultry, seafood, and fresh meat must be maintained at **4°C (40°F) or lower**.
- **Hot Holding**: Cooked hot foods designated for donation must be held at **60°C (140°F) or higher** until cooling or transport.
- **The Danger Zone**: The temperature range between **4°C and 60°C (40°F - 140°F)** fosters rapid bacterial proliferation. Food must not reside in the danger zone for more than **2 hours cumulative** (or 1 hour if ambient temperature exceeds 32°C / 90°F).

## 2. Safe Cooling and Reheating Protocols
- **Two-Stage Cooling Process**:
  1. Cool hot food from 60°C (140°F) down to 21°C (70°F) within **2 hours**.
  2. Cool from 21°C (70°F) down to 4°C (40°F) or below within an additional **4 hours** (total cooling window: 6 hours max).
- **Rapid Cooling Techniques**: Use shallow stainless steel pans (less than 2 inches / 5 cm deep), ice water baths with frequent stirring, or blast chillers.
- **Safe Reheating**: Previously cooked and chilled foods must be rapidly reheated to an internal temperature of at least **74°C (165°F)** for at least 15 seconds before serving.

## 3. High-Risk Food Guidelines
- **Cooked Grains (Rice & Pasta)**: *Bacillus cereus* spores survive boiling. Cooked rice and pasta must be cooled quickly in shallow trays and refrigerated immediately. Leftover cooked rice must never remain at room temperature.
- **Dairy and Soft Cheeses**: Keep strictly refrigerated. Discard any unpasteurized dairy surplus.
- **Prepared Salads and Cut Produce**: Cut leafy greens, sliced tomatoes, and melons must be kept at 4°C or below.

## 4. Packaging, Labeling, and Allergen Disclosures
- Every donated food package must bear a label stating:
  1. **Item Name & Description**
  2. **Preparation / Packaging Date and Time**
  3. **Major Allergen Disclosures**: Milk, Eggs, Fish, Crustacean Shellfish, Tree Nuts, Peanuts, Wheat, Soybeans, Sesame.
  4. **Storage Instructions**: "Keep Refrigerated at or below 4°C" or "Reheat to 74°C before eating".
- Food containers must be food-grade, leak-proof, clean, and tamper-evident.
