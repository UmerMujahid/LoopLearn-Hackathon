# Commercial Kitchen Food Waste Reduction Best Practices

## 1. Inventory Management & FIFO Optimization
- **First-In, First-Out (FIFO)**: Store older stock in front of newer stock. Label all incoming ingredients with delivery and opening dates.
- **Root-Cause Waste Auditing**: Measure and log kitchen trim waste, spoilage waste, and plate waste daily using a standard kitchen scale.
- **Storage Climate Control**: Keep ethylene-producing fruits (bananas, apples, tomatoes) separate from ethylene-sensitive greens and vegetables.

## 2. Production Planning & Batch Prep Strategies
- **Just-In-Time Batch Cooking**: Instead of cooking entire anticipated dinner services in one large batch, cook in staged 20-30 minute intervals based on real-time patron counts.
- **Cross-Utilization of Ingredients**: Repurpose surplus roasted vegetables into soups or purées; convert stale artisanal breads into croutons, bread pudding, or breadcrumbs.
- **Standardized Portioning Tools**: Use calibrated ladles, dishers, and scales during plating to prevent over-portioning plate waste.

## 3. Pre-Donation Surplus Segmentation
- **Tier 1 (Source Reduction)**: Adjust purchasing algorithms and order quantities based on weekly reservation forecasts.
- **Tier 2 (Feed Hungry People)**: Post unserved, safely held hot food, bakery surplus, and unblemished produce directly on the FoodLoop portal within 30 minutes of kitchen closing.
- **Tier 3 (Animal Feed & Composting)**: Channel inedible food scraps to licensed compost facilities or local agricultural farms.
